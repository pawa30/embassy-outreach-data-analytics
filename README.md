# Embassy Outreach Data Management & Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes an embassy outreach database to evaluate outreach progress, engagement levels, follow-up performance, regional patterns, and data quality. The objective is to transform raw records into actionable insights that support management decision-making.

The project uses Microsoft Excel to clean and organize data, build a management dashboard, identify data-quality issues, and develop recommendations for improving outreach tracking.

## Business Problem

Management needed a consolidated view of the outreach pipeline to identify:

- Embassies requiring follow-up.
- Engagement levels across outreach stages.
- Regional differences in engagement and follow-up performance.
- Uneven workload distribution among owners.
- Incomplete, inconsistent, and unreliable records.

## Tools & Skills

- **Microsoft Excel:** formulas, data cleaning, KPI calculations, dashboards, charts, and data-quality reviews.
- **Data Analysis:** pipeline analysis, regional comparisons, follow-up monitoring, and workload analysis.
- **Data Quality:** duplicate identification, missing-value checks, consistency checks, and record validation.
- **Business Analysis:** insight generation and actionable management recommendations.
- **AI-assisted analysis:** used to support planning, formula development, verification, and report writing, with results checked against the workbook.

## Project Highlights

The analysis covers 84 embassies across six regions, based on a reporting date of 6 October 2026.

Key findings include:

- **Outreach pipeline:** 59 of 84 embassies were in active outreach, while 19 had reached the Engaged stage.
- **Follow-up performance:** 30 of 59 active embassies were overdue for follow-up.
- **Engagement risks:** 13 active embassies had no contact for more than 90 days.
- **Data quality:** 53 of 84 records had at least one identified data gap or conflict.
- **Ownership and workload:** follow-up responsibilities were unevenly distributed, with some records lacking an assigned owner.

These findings informed recommendations for follow-up recovery, workload balancing, regional prioritization, and stronger data-entry controls.

| Dash_Calc | Supporting calculations for dashboard reporting |

## Recommendations

The analysis suggests several improvement areas:

1. Prioritize overdue follow-ups and high-engagement relationships.
2. Assign owners to unassigned records and rebalance workloads.
3. Review regional performance and stalled outreach stages.
4. Introduce mandatory fields and standardized dropdowns to improve data consistency.
5. Establish a recurring management review with measurable follow-up targets.
